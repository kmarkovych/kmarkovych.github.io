AutoSpend

The full picture of what your car costs — stored only on your phone.

Log a fill-up in seconds and AutoSpend works out your fuel economy from consecutive full tanks. Record services and repairs, attach receipt photos, set reminders by date or mileage, and export a clean PDF service book when it is time to sell the car.

Google Play App Store — coming soon

What it does

Built for the pump,
the garage and the sale

Fuel tracking
Price, volume and odometer per fill-up. Fuel economy in L/100 km or MPG, average fuel price, and spending trends over time.
Maintenance log
Oil changes, tyres, inspections and repairs, with notes and receipt photos attached to any entry.
Reminders
By calendar date or by mileage — whichever fits the job — with a notification when service is due.
PDF service book
Export the car's full history as a clean PDF. Useful at a new mechanic, essential when selling.
Multi-car garage
Several vehicles, each with its own history, statistics and reminders.
Your data
Stored locally, backed up to a file you choose, exportable to CSV. No account, no ads, no tracking.
